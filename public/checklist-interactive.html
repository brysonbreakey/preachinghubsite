Pre-Preaching Checklist

You've done the work. Before you step into the pulpit, walk through this once more — not to second-guess your prep, but to settle into it.

1Message Clarity
I can state my big idea in one sentence from memory
I know exactly where the gospel appears in this sermon
My application is specific — not just "trust God more" but a concrete action
2Opening & Closing
My first 60 seconds are memorized or nearly memorized
My closing lands on a clear call to response
I know how I'm ending before I start
3Flow & Transitions
I can move from point to point without looking at my notes
My illustrations support the point — they don't replace it
4Delivery Readiness
I've read this sermon aloud at least once
I know where I tend to rush and have marked those spots
I've prayed over this message and the people who will hear it
5Logistics
Notes or outline are ready and accessible
Any tech (slides, mic) is confirmed
